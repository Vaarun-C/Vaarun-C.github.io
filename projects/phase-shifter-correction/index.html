Model. A genetic search (a JavaScript port of the repo's objective, not its trained weights) picks one phase trim in 0 to 5.625° and the subset of states it applies to. Its score is the nearest-state RMS error over the 51-point band, and the 50 out-of-band points are a held-out check. The repo's measured S21 CSVs are gitignored, so the chip here is simulated from a bit-weight model; the repo's saved runs report RMS 0.78 to 6.14°.
